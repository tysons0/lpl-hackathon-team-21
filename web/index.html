How it works

  1. 1. Tell us your goalsChat or speak in your own words: what you're saving for, what worries you, and how you like to meet. There are no wrong answers.
  2. 2. Meet 3 matchesSee three advisors who fit your goals, language and schedule, with a plain-language reason for each one.
  3. 3. Walk in readyBook a time and get a personal prep kit: key terms explained simply, questions to ask, and what to bring. Your advisor gets a briefing too, so you start with your goals, not paperwork.

Free to use. We help you prepare, not invest: your advisor gives the advice.
